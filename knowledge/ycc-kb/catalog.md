# Yuzu Cigar Club Catalog Knowledge

Audience: adult Yuzu Cigar Club members and authorized operators.

Rules:
- Do not make health, medical, safety, or cessation claims about cigars or tobacco.
- Keep recommendations age-restricted and educational.
- Do not collect payment card data.
- When inventory, allocation, or fulfillment details are uncertain, hand off to a concierge operator.

Curated box catalog:

- Padron 1964 Anniversary Series: Nicaraguan Churchill, natural wrapper, medium strength, 7 inch by 50 ring gauge, box of 25. Tasting profile includes cocoa, coffee, oak, fig, leather, and almond. Store around 65 percent to 69 percent relative humidity and rotate the box monthly during the first quarter. Good pairings include barrel-proof bourbon, black coffee, and aged rum.
- Davidoff Signature No. 2: Dominican Panetela, Ecuador Connecticut-style wrapper, mild-medium strength, 6 inch by 38 ring gauge, box of 25. Tasting profile includes cream, cedar, almond, fresh hay, and white pepper. Keep closer to 65 percent relative humidity to preserve the draw and wrapper texture. Pair with espresso, Champagne, or green tea.
- Liga Privada T52: Nicaraguan Toro from Drew Estate, USA Connecticut Habano wrapper, full strength, 6 inch by 52 ring gauge, box of 24. Tasting profile includes cedar, mineral, molasses, pepper, and charred oak. Rest 45 days before opening the first cigar and store at 66 percent to 68 percent relative humidity.
- Plasencia Alma Fuerte Sixto I: Nicaraguan Hexagono, Nicaraguan Maduro-style wrapper, medium-full strength, 6 inch by 60 ring gauge, box of 20. Tasting profile includes dark chocolate, cedar, baking spice, earth, and dried fruit. Store at 67 percent to 69 percent relative humidity and avoid frequent lid openings while the Maduro wrapper settles.
- Arturo Fuente Hemingway: Dominican Perfecto, Cameroon wrapper, medium strength, 7 inch by 46 ring gauge, box of 25. Tasting profile includes cedar, sweet spice, toasted almond, orange peel, and cream. Store around 67 percent relative humidity and light patiently so the tapered foot opens evenly.
- Cohiba Serie M Reserva: USA-made Toro/BHK-style format, Dominican wrapper, medium-full strength, 6 inch by 52 ring gauge, box of 10. Member allocation item. Tasting profile includes cedar, citrus peel, cream, Corojo spice, and roasted nut. Store at 65 percent to 67 percent relative humidity and protect the wrapper from humidity swings.

General catalog guidance:

- Dark wrapper color does not always mean high nicotine strength.
- Wrapper leaf affects aroma and texture, while binder and filler determine balance and structure.
- Stronger cigars should be paced slowly and paired with water, coffee, tea, or spirits only when appropriate for the adult member.
- A useful cigar recommendation should mention wrapper, origin, vitola, strength, tasting notes, storage, and a human handoff when availability or compliance matters.
