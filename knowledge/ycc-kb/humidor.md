# Yuzu Cigar Club Humidor Knowledge

Audience: adult Yuzu Cigar Club members and authorized operators.

Rules:
- Humidor guidance is storage and quality guidance only.
- Do not imply cigars or tobacco are safe or healthy.
- Device, account, billing, and compliance issues should be routed to support.

Target ranges:

- YCC humidor cards use 65 percent to 72 percent relative humidity as the broad target.
- Many curated boxes are best managed around 65 percent to 69 percent relative humidity.
- The default smart hygrometer reading in the YCC experience is 69 percent humidity and 70 degrees Fahrenheit.
- Temperature target is usually 68 to 72 degrees Fahrenheit.

Storage principles:

- Stable conditions beat dramatic correction.
- Avoid frequent lid openings while boxes acclimate.
- Keep a calibrated hygrometer inside the storage area.
- Leave room for airflow around boxes.
- Group boxes by age, wrapper, origin, or smoking window.
- Track purchase date, rest date, first-smoke date, and next tasting date.

Climate signals:

- Too dry can show as brittle wrapper, hot burn, and thin smoke.
- Stable storage can show as clean draw, even burn, and clear aroma.
- Too wet can show as tight draw, muted flavor, and uneven burn.

Device support:

YCC supports manual entry, QR-based add, label scanning, humidity log, reorder reminders, and pairing notes. Smart hygrometer thermometer devices can connect by Bluetooth or WiFi. A valid device reading needs a name, location, identifier, humidity from 1 to 100, temperature from 40 to 95 degrees Fahrenheit, and a sync interval between 5 and 120 minutes.

Example inventory:

- Padron 1964 Anniversary Series: Nicaragua, natural wrapper, Locker A / Drawer 2, aging well.
- Davidoff Signature No. 2: Dominican Republic, Connecticut-style wrapper, Home / Tray 1.
- Liga Privada T52: Nicaragua, Habano wrapper, Locker B / Drawer 1.
- Plasencia Alma Fuerte Sixto I: Nicaragua, Maduro wrapper, Home / Box 3, aging well.
