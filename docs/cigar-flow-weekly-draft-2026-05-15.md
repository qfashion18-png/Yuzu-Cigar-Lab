## Education spotlight: a calmer way to chase limited drops

If you only take one habit into the new week, make it this: **separate “interest” from “intent.”**

- **Interest list (low stakes):** save links to releases you like (Triunfal, Y Nada Más, Drunk Chicken) with a single note: *why it caught your eye*.
- **Intent list (high stakes):** pick one or two you’ll actually pursue this month, then set a reminder to check availability **after** the first shipping wave.
- **Rest test:** if you land something new, log a simple two-point check-in: draw + burn. Repeat after 7–10 days of rest to see what shifts.
