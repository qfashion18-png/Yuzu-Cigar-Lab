# Yuzu Luxury Social Creative Standard

Date: 2026-06-08

Scope: Facebook-first organic content for Yuzu Cigar Club after the Instagram page was disabled.

## Channel Decision

- Active public channels: Facebook Page and Facebook Group.
- Disabled channel: Instagram. Do not require Instagram cross-posts in future scheduling or validation.
- Reusable vertical video is still useful for Facebook Reels, Facebook Stories, website embeds, and later reposting if Instagram access returns.

## Visual Standard

Yuzu posts should look like a luxury cigar magazine cover before they look like an information card.

Default image rules:

- Use full-bleed editorial photography with one obvious focal object.
- Keep dark lounge atmosphere, but preserve visible highlights and texture.
- Use champagne, ivory, brass, walnut, black leather, cedar, oxblood, and deep green as the material palette.
- Put the strongest object in the first frame: cigar ash, wrapper leaf, cutter, humidor drawer, journal, glassware, lounge chair, or cedar tray.
- Leave clean negative space for text. Do not stack text over busy image areas.
- Keep the Yuzu mark small and restrained.
- Avoid heavy black rectangles, dense bullet lists, glow blobs, stock-photo blur, and text that fills the whole image.

Text rules:

- Cover headline: 4 to 7 words.
- Cover subline: 6 to 10 words.
- Body slides: one idea per slide.
- Use bullets only on inner infographic slides, never the cover.
- Favor concrete questions over broad slogans.

## 2026-06-09 Detail Upgrade

Education posts should not ship as a short caption plus one vague cover image. Use one of these formats instead:

- Preferred Page format: a visual guide set with one cover card and 3 to 5 step/detail cards.
- Story companion: education guides promoted in Stories need native 1080x1920 layouts. Do not stretch or crop the 4:5 feed cards; render a separate Story-safe sequence with top and bottom text lifted away from Story UI.
- Acceptable fallback: one detailed 4:5 visual guide image with clear step labels, plus the source package needed to make follow-up cards.
- Group format: a discussion prompt with at least one visual detail card, diagram, or step image before posting.

Caption structure for education posts:

- Lead with the practical hook.
- Give 3 to 5 concrete instructions or cues.
- Add one common mistake or reset step.
- End with a specific comment question.
- Close with adult 21+ and no-marketplace framing.

Visual structure for education posts:

- Show the object or action, not only a mood photo.
- Add simple visual cues: arrows, numbered steps, tray/rest/cutter/humidor diagrams, or close-up callouts.
- Keep every text block inside its panel with visible margin at mobile-feed size.
- Use a contact sheet for internal QA when a post has multiple cards.
- Use a Story contact sheet for vertical sequences and visually check long titles, footer/legal text, and sticker/reply-safe spacing before publishing.
- Final public assets must use premium photorealistic or editorial photography as the visual layer. Avoid clipart, vector-style diagrams, cartoon objects, flat icons, fake arrows as the main visual, CGI-looking cigars, and low-detail instructional graphics. Use overlays for typography and restrained callouts only.

Recommended dimensions:

- Facebook Page feed: `1080x1350` or `1200x1500` 4:5 when possible.
- Facebook Group: `1080x1080` square prompt card or album crop.
- Facebook Reels/Stories: `1080x1920` vertical image sequence or MP4.
- Link previews: `1200x628` only when a landscape preview is specifically needed.

## Month Content Pillars

1. **Technique Infographics**
   - How to ash a cigar.
   - How to cut a cigar.
   - How to toast and light.
   - How to read ring gauge and shape.

2. **Wrapper And Anatomy Education**
   - Wrapper color meaning.
   - Wrapper, binder, filler roles.
   - Strength vs body vs flavor.
   - Ash color: useful observation, not a total review.

3. **Humidor And Notes**
   - Humidor consistency.
   - What to track.
   - Resting cigars over time.
   - Travel humidor checklist.

4. **Lounge Culture**
   - Lounge etiquette.
   - Patio etiquette.
   - Pairing notes.
   - Beginner questions without snobbery.

5. **Cigar Flow Editorial**
   - Weekly release/culture signals.
   - Maker heritage.
   - Collector design.
   - What deserves the deeper read.

6. **Trend-Safe Short-Form Ideas**
   - Emotional recreations: quick reaction cuts to everyday cigar-care moments, such as ash dropping early, a humidor finally stabilizing, or discovering a tasting note. Use original audio or clean licensed audio only.
   - Rough-week humor: low-stakes adult coping framed around organizing the humidor, cleaning the ashtray, logging tasting notes, or choosing a quiet lounge chair. Do not imply tobacco as stress treatment or health support.
   - Mythical visual effects: tasteful Cigar Flow transitions from a real lounge table into a surreal news-map or humidor-world visual. Keep the luxury texture intact and avoid glitch clutter.
   - Reaction streams: split-screen commentary for educational myths, wrapper assumptions, or etiquette questions. Use as Page Reels or Group discussion prompts.

## Asset Prompt Pattern

Use this structure for image generation:

```text
Create a premium editorial photograph for an adult 21+ cigar education post.
No text, no logos, no people, no readable labels.
Scene: [specific cigar/luxury setup].
Composition: full-bleed 4:5, focal object in one third, clean negative space for headline.
Style: quiet luxury, warm champagne key light, black leather, walnut, cedar, brass, shallow depth of field.
Quality: photorealistic, crisp foreground detail, bright enough focal highlights, no over-dark murk, no watermark.
```

Overlay text after generation using local layout code so the typography stays sharp, editable, and compliant.

## Video Standard

- First second: object motion or reveal, not a title card.
- Best starts: humidor drawer opening, ash macro, cutter glint, wrapper leaf pan, journal note reveal, or cigar rest close-up.
- Duration: 12 to 24 seconds for most education videos.
- Every video must include a clean audio track unless explicitly silent.
- Verify audio before publishing:
  - AAC stereo or mono at normal social bitrate.
  - No clipping.
  - No static-like high-frequency signature.
  - No long dead tail.
  - No synthetic tone/drone beds that can transcode into buzz.

## Compliance Guardrails

- Always include adult `21+ only` framing in public copy.
- No marketplace language: no buying, selling, trading, pricing, inventory, order requests, discounts, giveaways, samples, or direct product availability.
- No health, safer, therapeutic, low-risk, or modified-risk claims.
- Education posts should be descriptive and subjective where appropriate, not absolute claims about quality or health.
