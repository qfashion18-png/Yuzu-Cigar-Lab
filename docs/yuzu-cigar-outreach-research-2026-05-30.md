## Outreach Rules

Use this as a practical operating guardrail, not legal advice.

1. Treat outreach as commercial email. Use accurate From/Reply-To, truthful subject lines, clear sender identity, physical mailing address, and a working opt-out. Honor opt-outs within 10 business days. Source: `https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business`
2. Keep all tobacco-related messaging adult-only. Federal Tobacco 21 applies to cigars and sales to anyone under 21. Source: `https://www.fda.gov/tobacco-products/retail-sales-tobacco-products/tobacco-21`
3. Do not buy lists, scrape member lists, harvest private/personal emails, or DM individual group members. Use public business emails, official contact forms, opt-in contacts, or professional role contacts.
4. Do not say or imply cigars are safer, healthy, clean, light, low-risk, or otherwise reduced-risk unless a specific FDA-authorized modified-risk claim exists. Source: `https://www.fda.gov/tobacco-products/advertising-and-promotion/modified-risk-tobacco-products`
5. Avoid `free cigar`, `free sample`, or giveaway wording in cold outreach. Use partnership, event, member access, or media inquiry language instead. Source: `https://www.fda.gov/tobacco-products/products-guidance-regulations/advertising-and-promotion`
6. Do not promise nationwide shipping in cold outreach. Shipping, taxes, licenses, delivery-sale rules, and carrier policies vary by state and product. Sources: `https://www.atf.gov/alcohol-tobacco/prevent-all-cigarette-trafficking-pact-act` and `https://www.cdc.gov/statesystem/`
7. For creators/media, require disclosure if anything of value changes hands: compensation, product, affiliate value, hosted event access, or comped membership. Source: `https://www.ftc.gov/influencers`

## Spreadsheet Schema

The CSV uses these columns:

- `lead_name`
- `type`
- `priority`
- `geography`
- `public_contact`
- `contact_method`
- `website_or_profile`
- `source_url`
- `fit_for_yuzu`
- `outreach_angle`
- `compliance_notes`
- `score`
- `status`

Status meanings:

- `ready`: usable public/business contact path found.
- `verify`: promising lead, but confirm current activity, organizer, or event continuity before sending.
- `contact_form_first`: use the official form or generic business contact rather than named personal emails from third-party directories.
- `low_priority`: credible but competitor-adjacent, less audience-fit, or no clean direct contact.

## Scoring Rubric

Score out of 100:

- Audience fit: 25 points for adult cigar audience, community strength, and Yuzu relevance.
- Compliance quality: 25 points for public business contact, 21+ positioning, no scraped/private source, and low platform risk.
- Commercial fit: 20 points for lounge, club, retailer, event host, creator, or media partnership potential.
- Engagement signal: 15 points for recent posts, events, newsletter, reviews, or community activity.
- Reputation fit: 15 points for professional tone, premium positioning, and no youth-oriented branding.

Use leads scoring 70+ for the first two waves. Keep low-priority leads for later manual review.
