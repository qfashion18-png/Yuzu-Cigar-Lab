# Design System

## Overview

This explainer teaches the Yuzu Cigar Club Digital Humidor as a premium member tool. It should feel like a guided product walkthrough inside a private cigar lounge: dark, quiet, instructional, and dashboard focused. The product UI is the hero, with gold callouts, serif chapter titles, compact stats, and realistic cigar box imagery.

## Colors

- Night Background: `#030504`
- Ink Surface: `#07110D`
- Card Surface: `#101812`
- Forest Surface: `#0A1C14`
- Accent Gold: `#DCA93A`
- Gold Highlight: `#F3C86C`
- Cream Text: `#F8EDD7`
- Muted Text: `#B8AA8F`
- Line Gold: `#DCA93A57`
- Success Green: `#5FB56A`
- Warning Red: `#E55B48`

## Typography

- Display Serif: Georgia, `"Times New Roman"`, serif for scene titles and emotional product statements.
- Interface Sans: Arial, ui-sans-serif, system-ui for labels, steps, captions, and operational copy.
- Numbers use tabular figures in stats, climate readings, counters, and timelines.

## Motion

- Use calm luxury pacing with blur crossfades, focus pulls, subtle screenshot drift, gold rule drawing, stat counters, and guided callout highlights.
- Every scene gets staggered entrances. No element should pop fully formed.
- Transitions should mostly be blur crossfades, with one vertical push for the topic change from overview into data entry.
- Keep the final scene simple: recap, clear next action, and a soft fade to black.

## What NOT To Do

- Do not use novelty smoke, cartoon cigar icons, bright gradients, neon colors, or generic SaaS blue.
- Do not make the video text-only; the real humidor dashboard and product cards should stay visible often.
- Do not cover the central dashboard with large presenter graphics.
- Do not over-round cards. Use small radius, thin gold borders, and dense member-dashboard layouts.
