# Storyboard

Format: 1080x1920 vertical mobile
Duration target: 61 seconds
Audio: warm guided voiceover with restrained lounge-product pacing
Style basis: DESIGN.md, existing Yuzu product promo and digital humidor assets

## Beat 1 - Invitation Hook (0.00-7.05s)

Voiceover: "Yuzu Cigar Club helps adults buy, track, and enjoy premium cigars with less guesswork."

Visual: Full-frame lounge/box photography with Yuzu logo, a large Georgia headline, and three compact proof chips: curated boxes, member access, digital humidor.

Transition: Focus-pull blur crossfade into membership value.

## Beat 2 - Why Membership (6.65-15.55s)

Voiceover: "Membership unlocks member-cost cigar boxes, private drops, curated monthly cigars on select tiers, concierge guidance, and member events."

Visual: Membership-box photography and product cards stack upward. Gold tags lock in for member-cost boxes, private drops, monthly cigars, and concierge.

Transition: Vertical push into the tier stack.

## Beat 3 - What You Get (15.10-27.05s)

Voiceover: "Pick the level that fits your cellar. Box Access Pass gives you box pricing and digital humidor access. Kisha, Sensei, and Daimyo add four, eight, or twelve curated cigars, plus stronger allocations and support."

Visual: Four tier rows rise into a premium membership panel. Tier details show the core value: access pass, 4 cigars, 8 cigars, 12 cigars.

Transition: Warm light-leak crossfade into the humidor interface.

## Beat 4 - Digital Humidor (26.60-40.05s)

Voiceover: "The digital humidor is your collection dashboard. Add cigars by photo, QR, label scan, or manual entry. Track quantity, location, humidity, temperature, aging windows, tasting notes, and reorder reminders."

Visual: Phone/dashboard screenshots fan in. A gold loop connects five steps: add, track, age, note, reorder. Climate stats count to 69% and 70 F.

Transition: Focus-pull to the ready/rest/replace decision moment.

## Beat 5 - Smoke Smarter (39.60-47.05s)

Voiceover: "It helps you know what is ready to smoke, what should rest, and what to replace before it runs low."

Visual: Three decision cards appear over cigar record/product imagery: ready to smoke, keep resting, reorder soon.

Transition: Color dip to the trust and feature recap.

## Beat 6 - Other Features and Trust (46.60-56.05s)

Voiceover: "Yuzu also supports age verification, destination rules, adult-signature delivery, orders, billing, support, and member-only releases."

Visual: Checkout and account-style panels with shield, truck, calendar, and support feature tiles. Compliance is presented as a member trust layer.

Transition: Slow blur crossfade into the logo close.

## Beat 7 - Close (55.60-61.00s)

Voiceover: "Join Yuzu Cigar Club. Build a box-worthy cellar with the tools to manage it."

Visual: Yuzu logo centered over a faint collage of product boxes and humidor UI. CTA bar: Join Now, Shop Boxes, Explore Humidor. Final fade to night background.
