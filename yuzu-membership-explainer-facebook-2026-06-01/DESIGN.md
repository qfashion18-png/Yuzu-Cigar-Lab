# Design System

## Overview

Yuzu Membership Explainer uses the existing Yuzu Cigar Club private-lounge identity in a mobile-first vertical format. This Facebook-safe variant should feel like a guided adult 21+ member invitation: premium, dark, warm, practical, and clearly tied to membership, digital humidor tools, concierge guidance, events, and compliance trust signals.

## Colors

- Night Background: `#030504`
- Ink Surface: `#07110D`
- Card Surface: `#101812`
- Forest Surface: `#0A1C14`

- Gold Highlight: `#F3C86C`

- Muted Text: `#B8AA8F`

- Success Green: `#5FB56A`
- Compliance Red: `#E55B48`

## Typography

- Display Serif: Georgia, `"Times New Roman"`, serif for the invitation headline, membership promise, and closing line.
- Interface Sans: Arial, ui-sans-serif, system-ui for captions, feature labels, tier details, and dashboard UI.
- Numeric UI uses tabular figures for climate, inventory, tier pricing, and checklist details.

## Layout

- Format: 1080x1920 vertical mobile video.
- Scenes use full-frame photographic or app imagery with dark green overlays, small-radius panels, thin gold rules, and large readable type.
- Keep the brand mark visible early, then use UI proof: membership page, product boxes, humidor dashboard, journal, checkout, and feature cards.
- Text must be short enough to read on mobile: one major statement per beat, supported by two to four compact labels.

## Motion

- Use calm premium pacing with blur crossfades, focus pulls, vertical pushes, gold rule drawing, stat counters, and subtle parallax.
- Every scene has entrance animations. Transitions, not element exits, carry scene changes.
- Final scene may fade to near black after the call to action.

## Do's

- Use cigar box photography and real Yuzu UI screenshots as proof.
- Present membership as a practical club layer: curated cigar experiences, concierge, events, education, support, and humidor tools.
- Explain the digital humidor as a loop: add cigars, monitor climate, age boxes, write notes, and reorder when needed.
- Keep age verification, destination rules, and adult-signature delivery as trust signals.

## Don'ts

- Do not imply cigars or tobacco are healthy or risk-free.
- Do not use pricing, checkout, order, inventory, discount, drop, or direct purchase language in the Facebook variant.
- Do not use novelty smoke, cartoons, neon colors, or generic tech gradients.
- Do not make compliance feel like legal fine print; make it a secure member workflow.
- Do not cover app screenshots with oversized text.
